Realcommercial Home

How might we design a more engaging experience that brings the user back to the app over and over again?

01.-

Background

Realcommercial is a marketplace for people to find commercial properties to buy or lease. The app provides people to search and save properties on the go. Being the No.1 app for commercial properties, the business takes app traffic seriously. 'Time in app' and 'app launches' are two key metrics measured against the competitor.

02.-

The challenge

Increase app launches

As a team, we started to brainstorm what we could do to increase app traffic, specifically to increase app launches as a first step.
From previous testing sessions and analytics data, I found out users often use the recent searches feature to check back every week to see if there’s anything new. It saves time to fire a new search. There is an opportunity there to encourage users to set search notifications that would lead to more app launches.

User research

From observing them using the app and talking to users, many users didn’t realise they can save their recent search parameter to receive an update if there's a new property that comes onto the market that fits their criteria. I saw an opportunity for the UI uplift for the recent searches cards on the homescreen and updating the inline text link to an actual button. It would increase the visibility of the button.

Hypotheses

I also set up 2 hypotheses to measure the success of the change.

1. If we update the set alert button UI, people are more likely to click on it because the current button is not visible to some users.
2. More alerts would lead to more app launches and an increase on time in app.

03.-

The impact

Early signs

The team implemented the UI change on iOS first and we saw a huge increase in notifications set. Even though December is usually a quiet month, the amount of alerts set was significant (pretty much double!). To make sure we’re not just getting more notifications in general, separating the context by screens in Mixpanel showed us the impact of the change on home screen.

Keep implementing, keep tracking

A month after seeing the positive impact on ios app, we also implemented on Android and saw good results. Even though during COVID-19 the usage dropped a bit, overall it was still much higher than last year.

05.-

The outcome

25% increase on app launches YoY

Not only the result validated the first hypothesis, we also saw a 25% increase on app launches compared to last year. The team got a mention by the CEO during the company wide update!

04.-

Looking ahead

Design vision

It was exciting to see the impact of UI uplift! It really motivated the team to see the impact of their work. The change didn't stop there. The next question is, now that we have seen an increase in app launches, how can we keep our users in the app?

I started looking at how we can support our users better with more personalised content as a design vision. The idea is to serve relevant content based on user search behaviour and to suggest similar properties, similar suburbs to explore, and relevant tips in the commercial property market.

Adding delight to the home screen with animated illustration and bringing the recent search card higher.

Show suggested properties based on previous search and provide recently viewed properties in case user wants to revisit what they saw.

Relevant commercial properties news & tips based on previous search.

Suggest similar suburbs to expand the search range.
