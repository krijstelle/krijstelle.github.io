28 By Sam Wood

Building a fitness app in 6 months.

01.-

Background

28 by Sam Wood is a successful fitness program with over 150,000 users. The program includes a daily workout, daily recipes, shopping list, and mindfulness resources.

The project goal was not only to turn Sam's existing program into an app but also to ensure the experience is personal and flexible.

From the business perspective, having an app allowed Sam to provide a more engaging experience to his users. With features like notifications, Sam was able to stay connected with his users and help them to stay motivated throughout the week. It also unlocked other mobile features such as camera for people to track their progress.

My role

I worked closely with a UX lead, the delivery team and stakeholders to solve problems and came up with design solutions for each sprint.
I built and maintained a component library to streamline the workflow between design and development. I also led the usability testing, to hear from our users and turned their feedback into actionable items to share with the team.

02.-

Key learnings from trying the web program

New users need help

For new users entering into the program, there was a lot of information to take in on the first week. Because it's so comprehensive, they can feel a bit overwhelmed. The app had the opportunity to break all the information into digestible chunks.

Eating out?

There wasn’t a way for people to swap or skip meals. There needs to be a way for people to mark the meal as skip or swap recipes.

Shopping list

Shopping for the entire week is not something everyone does. Having the flexibility to choose what meals to shop for is important.

Scaling the recipe

Many users don't just cook for themselves, but having to manually scale the recipe when cooking for the household can be a lot of work. If the app can do the math, it would save them a lot of trouble.

03.-

Collaboration

Component library

Due to the short timeframe we had for delivery, part of my role in this project was to put together a component library to speed up the handover process. The component library followed the Atomic design methodology to define the basics as the first step, like colours, typography and buttons.

04.-

User feedback

Recommendations from usability testing

Even though the project was under a very strict timeline, I managed to do some testings with real users before completion and put together some recommendations for the team before product launch.
• Observation: It took time for people to get used to the app architecture and learn the basics.
• Swipe to complete gesture is not intuitive.
• The icon for select/ deselect meals to shop for can be confusing.

I took the opportunity to improve the web experience to bring parity between the two platforms. I also worked with the team on onboarding to provide first-time users some quick tips.

05.-

The outcome

The app has become the core part of Sam Wood's program. It was the #1 downloaded app in Health & Fitness(AU) in 2019 and was featured as 'App of the day' in Appstore.
