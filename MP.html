A disruption to an old industry

In America, there’s the National School Lunch Program where every school kids gets free lunch. It is every meal planner’s challenge to make sure every day the school serves meals that is compliant, so that the school can get reimbursed from the government. Working a meal planner can be stressful, as it is a big job to ensure kids love their lunches and schools serves the meal according to the standard to get reimbursed.

Currently meal planners work closely with dietician, using excel spreadsheet for calculation, to make sure all meals are compliance with USDA standards and regulations.

My Role

I was the main designer in this project together with another designer, in a team of 3 front-end developers, 2 back-end developers, 1 BA/producer. My role as a product designer involves early stages research, rapid prototyping, workshop facilitation for story planning, information architecture, as well as building and maintaining a living component library.

Test often and early

Testing in early stage of the development cycle was so valuable as the results uncovered many aspected of the challenge that I didn’t consider at the first place. As our target users are not very tech-savvy, the first version of design was a bit overwhelming to them. I had to make sure the page is straight forward and let the user take one step at a time.

Agile and UX

Working on this project has given me a deep insight of how UX fits into the agile process. Every sprint we retro and improve our process. Gradually we no longer just race for the next sprint, but having a long term horizon envisiong how the prodcut will look like. Due to the timeline and complexity of this project, the team prioritised 'features' over 'pixel-perfect UI' for version 1. While that allowed design to continue working on the long term vision, I also worked on a UI overhaul in the background, based on upgrading current component library.

Cinema Friday

Getting the whole team to watch the user test gives them a sense of involvement and seeing how the product they built being used by real human was great! It also allows the team to receive direct feedback from the user and bring the whole team into the UX journey.

Design studio

Due to the complexity of this project, there are times the engineers spend long hours working on calculation and features to meet regulation. I would borrow an hour of everyone’s time once a week to involve the whole team in some design problems - such as doing a design studio of the 404 error page. Such activities bring the whole team’s creative mind together and everyone felt more energised after solving some fun design problems together.
