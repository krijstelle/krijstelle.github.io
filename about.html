My design process

My design process typically starts from learning about the user, then mapping out the journey and identifying what problem we’re solving before diving into any pixels.
One of my strengths is facilitating sketching workshops to bring everyone’s idea together and allow iterating faster.

To begin with, I would start with user research and make sure I fully understand the problem by diving into user needs and business goals.

I then would roll out my wireframing dough to make some quick and rough prototypes for discussion, and making sure everyone is on the same page.

Testing with the user helps me to understand whether or not I have achieved my goal and also getting feedback for further iteration.

Then I would spice it up with more details. I work closely with the team to make sure everything is implemented as expected.

Illustration

In my spare time, I enjoy doing illustration. I went through different phases exploring different techniques in digital and on canvas. Lately I've been focusing more on surface design and working towards making my prints into products.
