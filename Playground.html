Playground

Incorporating animation into my projects is one of my favorite ways to add a delightful touch. I use different techniques and tools in each project depending on the needs and collaborating with engineers to bring design and code to life. In my spare time, I also try to apply animation to my personal illustration projects.

Lottie animation

I use After Effect or Hiku to create microinteractions and export them into Lottie files. It allows a smooth handover to developers where design deos the heavy lifting. Empty states, loading states, errors states and buttons are perfect examples using this method.

Screen transition

I use Protopie, Framer or Principle to create transition animation. The handover process for this type of animation is not as straightforward compared to Lottie. I would pair with the engineer in my team to achieve the ddesiredoutcome.

Illustrations

In my spare time, I also experimented with adding subtle animations to add a bit of playfulness to my illustrations.

AR animations

Another experiment to my illustration was to turn them into AR and incorporate real life background.
