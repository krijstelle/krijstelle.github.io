Breathe-O'Clock

Exploring the possibility of encouraging deep breathing through product movements.

01.-

Background

The project was done in 2012 during my honour year of industrial design as part of my graduate thesis, supervised by Dr Scott Mitchell. Throughout the year I went through user research, several rounds of user testing, and prototyping using microcontroller Arduino board. ​
The goal is to create awareness of deep breathing rhythm in everyday life, which leads to relaxation and stress relief.

02.-

Design process

Project overview

Throughout the year, the project had 3 milestones. Each milestone would involve a thesis check-in, form and material testing, and tech prototype testing. ​

03.-

Prototyping

The making...and lots lots testings

I explored different paper folding techniques to simulate the deep breathing movements of humany body. To be honest it was quite challenging learning different patterns while finding the right fit.

For the tech side, I experimented with different electronics, like servo motors, muscle wire and tried out different code to mimic the breathing rhythm.
